# Prompts used for the bridges (Google Flow, Veo 3.1 Lite, 8 s, 16:9, first frame + last frame)

Each bridge was generated with two attached images: the last frame of the scene before it as the first frame,
and a frame near the start of the scene after it as the last frame.

## Bridge 1: Marina by day → night road

One continuous shot, no cuts. The lime-yellow Lamborghini Urus keeps driving forward along the road the whole
time, wheels turning. The camera glides smoothly from the front three-quarter view round to the left side of the
car and moves a little closer, ending in a side tracking shot. During the shot the day turns into night: the
daylight fades through a short sunset into a deep-blue night sky, street lamps and building lights come on, and
the towers and palm trees beside the boulevard slide away behind the car and give way to a wide highway with the
lit Dubai skyline and Burj Khalifa in the distance. The change of place and light is gradual and physically
believable, like a time-lapse drive - never a cut, a fade to black or a flash. The car keeps exactly the same
shape, colour, wheels and details the whole time. No text, no logos, no people, no other cars.

## Bridge 2: night road → rental lot in the morning (second take, the one in the film)

A single uninterrupted camera move with no cuts, no cross-fades and no dissolves. The lime-yellow Lamborghini Urus
drives forward along the road the whole time and never turns around. The camera stays on the car's left side, a
little ahead of it, for the entire shot and never goes behind the car: we always see the car's front-left
three-quarter view, never its rear. The camera slowly pulls back and rises slightly, so the car becomes smaller
and moves to the right part of the frame. The scenery changes only because the car and the camera travel on: the
night skyline falls behind, the sky brightens from night through a pink dawn to clear morning light, the street
lamps go out, and the road leads into a paved car park in front of a modern glass building with palm trees, where
a long, neat row of identical white sedans comes into view on the left. The car's shape, colour, wheels and
details stay exactly the same in every frame. No text, no logos, no people.

(The first take used the looser wording of bridge 1 and showed the car from behind half-way through.)

## Bridge 3: rental lot → desert road

One continuous shot, no cuts. The lime-yellow Lamborghini Urus pulls away and keeps driving forward the whole
time, wheels turning, gathering speed. The camera moves back ahead of the car and drifts a little to the left, so
the car becomes smaller and sits in the right part of the frame. During the shot the place changes around the
car: the parked white cars, the palm trees and the glass building slide away behind it, the paving turns into an
open asphalt road, and the city gives way to a wide, empty desert with tall golden sand dunes under a hazy warm
sky; a little dust starts to rise behind the wheels. The change of place is gradual and physically believable,
like a time-lapse drive out of the city - never a cut, a fade or a flash. The car keeps exactly the same shape,
colour, wheels and details the whole time. No text, no logos, no people.

## Bridge 4: desert road → Downtown plaza at dusk

One continuous shot, no cuts. The lime-yellow Lamborghini Urus keeps driving forward the whole time, wheels
turning, slowing down towards the end. The camera stays in front of the car and drifts slightly towards the car's
left side, ending in a front three-quarter view. During the shot the day turns into dusk and the place changes
around the car: the sun goes down, the sky deepens to blue hour, the sand dunes sink away behind the car, the
desert road becomes a wide polished stone plaza that reflects the lights, and behind the car the lit towers of
Downtown Dubai with Burj Khalifa rise into view; the dust settles and the car's headlights glow. The change of
place and light is gradual and physically believable, like a time-lapse drive into the city - never a cut, a fade
to black or a flash. The car keeps exactly the same shape, colour, wheels and details the whole time. No text, no
logos, no people, no other cars.
